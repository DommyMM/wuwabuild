How this reads the export

A loss is an event.
A guarantee is its consequence.

Direct win: a limited resonator appears while your next five-star is still a live 50/50.

Loss: one of the standard resonators appears, turning the next limited five-star into a guarantee.

Guarantee: that next limited resonator pays the debt. It belongs in your five-star total, but not in the denominator of your coin-flip win rate.

The moving average also excludes guarantees. That makes it answer one clean question: among recent chances to win or lose, which happened more often?
